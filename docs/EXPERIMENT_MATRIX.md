Generated by `python run_campaign.py report`. Do not edit by hand:
the source of truth is `configs/campaign.yaml`.

Each row maps an item from the examination board's revision plan
(*Planejamento de ajustes na tese*, May 2026) to the experiment that
answers it.

## E1_main_grid — Main acquisition grid with sweet-spot search (H1, H3)

**Kind:** `al_grid`  
**Planned runs:** 480  
**Priority:** 1

**Addresses:**

- Sec.5: incluir experimentos com L0 < 100 e batches menores (B=32 vs B=50)
- Sec.5: encontrar o sweet spot do tamanho do batch e de L0
- Sec.5: incluir mais execucoes experimentais e validacao estatistica robusta
- Sec.5: abordar explicitamente a promocao de diversidade na selecao
- H1, H3

**Why this experiment:**

The preliminary results covered L0 in {100,200,300,400} at B=50 with a single run. Figure 15 showed the curves stabilising from L0>=200, which means the interesting regime is below 100 and was never sampled. This block extends the grid downward to L0=25 and adds B=32, and repeats every cell over n_seeds so differences can be tested rather than eyeballed. Diversity diagnostics are recorded per cycle, which is what turns H3 from an accuracy comparison into direct evidence about the selection mechanism.

## E2a_shift_baseline — No-adaptation baseline under covariate shift (H2 reference)

**Kind:** `shift_baseline`  
**Planned runs:** 20  
**Priority:** 1

**Addresses:**

- Cronograma Sem.3: rodar baseline de shift, registrar queda de recall e mAP@50
- H2

**Why this experiment:**

Every claim about adaptation under shift needs the counterfactual: how far the source-trained model falls on the target domain when nothing is done. Without this row the adaptation curves have no zero point, and the thesis cannot quantify how much of the final recall is recovery versus baseline competence.

## E2b_shift_real — Cross-dataset covariate shift, all strategies (H2, C1)

**Kind:** `shift_grid`  
**Planned runs:** 80  
**Priority:** 1

**Addresses:**

- Sec.5: incluir experimentos com distribution shift / data shift
- H2 (robustez sob distribution shift) - ainda nao verificada experimentalmente
- C1 (arquitetura adaptativa sob distribution shifts)
- Cronograma Sem.3-4: Bayesiana vs deterministica sob shift

**Why this experiment:**

H2 and C1 are stated in the thesis but were never tested: the shift code existed and produced per-strategy files, yet the combined table was empty. Real source->target pairs are used rather than only synthetic augmentation, because the board's concern is a genuine change of domain (different sensors, altitudes and scenes), which brightness and fog jitter does not reproduce. Bayesian and deterministic estimators are run on identical splits so the stability comparison is paired.

## E2c_shift_intensity — Graded synthetic shift intensity (H2 dose-response)

**Kind:** `shift_grid`  
**Planned runs:** 40  
**Priority:** 2

**Addresses:**

- Sec.5.3.6.3 da tese: intensificacao controlada do shift
- Sec.9: robustez do limiar tau_shift sob intensificacao do shift

**Why this experiment:**

Cross-dataset pairs show whether the method survives a shift; they cannot show how it degrades as the shift grows, because the intensity is not a controllable variable. The synthetic ladder supplies that axis and is the only way to demonstrate that the shift-detection threshold triggers at the right point instead of firing arbitrarily.

## E3_uncertainty_methods — Uncertainty estimator comparison and cost (C2, board Sec.9.1-9.2)

**Kind:** `uncertainty_comparison`  
**Planned runs:** 40  
**Priority:** 2

**Addresses:**

- Sec.9.1: MC Dropout e Bayesiano *aproximado*; justificar pelo custo
- Sec.9.1: verificar se o custo do MC Dropout e praticavel na borda
- Sec.9.1: reavaliar Deep Ensembles (5 modelos), custo vs ganho
- Sec.9.1: considerar predicao conformal como alternativa caixa-preta
- Sec.9.1: explorar SWAG
- Sec.9.2: experimentar BSB/PSB; abordagem de portfolio de criterios

**Why this experiment:**

The board questioned both the label ("Bayesiano" should be "Bayesiano aproximado") and the choice itself. Answering the second requires the alternatives measured on the same pipeline and the same splits, with their costs attached: every method here reports accuracy alongside acquisition-time overhead, resident memory and storage, so the thesis can defend MC Dropout on the cost-benefit argument rather than asserting it.

## E5_long_horizon — Long-horizon adaptation and catastrophic forgetting

**Kind:** `al_grid`  
**Planned runs:** 6  
**Priority:** 2

**Addresses:**

- Sec.5: tratar esquecimento catastrofico e adaptacao a novos conceitos
- Sec.3: 'aprendizado continuo' - aprofundar adequadamente ou reduzir destaque
- Tese Sec.5.3.6.3: estender K alem de 8

**Why this experiment:**

The thesis invokes Continual Learning, and the board's position was that it must either be treated properly or de-emphasised. This block takes the first option: K is extended to 20 cycles and the model is evaluated on every source domain after every cycle, which yields the backward-transfer and retention numbers the claim requires. It also answers the separate question of whether the BALD+Diversity advantage survives as the model matures, or erodes once the easy gains are exhausted.

## E6_operational_profiling — Extended operational profiling (H4, C3, C4)

**Kind:** `profiling`  
**Planned runs:** 6  
**Priority:** 1

**Addresses:**

- H4: medir apenas latencia e insuficiente - ampliar para memoria, trafego de rede, processamento e energia
- C3 (edge-native): detalhar e demonstrar o que torna o pipeline edge-native
- C4: ampliar a validacao alem da medicao de latencia
- Sec.5: incluir ou planejar concretamente o profiling em hardware embarcado real

**Why this experiment:**

H4 rested on a single mean latency measured on a workstation, which the board correctly called insufficient. This block measures the four resources it named, separates deployment cost (one deterministic pass, on the real-time path) from acquisition cost (T stochastic passes, off it), and reports the latency distribution rather than the mean, because the real-time claim is decided by the tail. The same script runs on a Jetson without modification and reads tegrastats rail power there; until that run happens the results declare the platform they were measured on, so no workstation number can be mistaken for an embedded one.
